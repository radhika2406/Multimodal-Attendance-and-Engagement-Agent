In educational institutions and professional training environments, attendance tracking remains largely manual or limited to basic biometric systems that fail to capture meaningful engagement and participation context. These traditional approaches are time-consuming, prone to inaccuracies, and provide no insight into learner attention, interaction patterns, or session effectiveness. As classrooms increasingly adopt digital tools, there is a growing need for intelligent systems that can move beyond simple presence detection.To address this challenge, the Multimodal Attendance & Engagement Agent was developed as an AI-powered, privacy-preserving classroom intelligence system. The project integrates computer vision, audio signal processing, and agent-based reasoning to automatically track attendance, analyze engagement trends, generate AI-driven teaching suggestions, and provide student session analytics. Designed to operate entirely offline, the system ensures ethical deployment while offering actionable insights that support educators and learners.

By providing unprecedented, actionable insights into the dynamics of classroom engagement, the Multimodal Attendance & Engagement Agent empowers educators to adapt their teaching methodologies in real-time and supports learners in optimizing their academic performance, ultimately creating a more effective and personalized learning experience.

Scenarios

Scenario 1: Automated Attendance Tracking
During a classroom session, the system processes a live or recorded video feed to detect and track participants. Attendance is marked based on sustained presence rather than single-frame detection, reducing false positives caused by temporary appearances. Timestamped attendance logs are generated automatically, eliminating manual roll calls and improving accuracy.

Scenario 2: Engagement and Session Dynamics Analysis
The system continuously analyzes behavioral indicators such as head pose, movement patterns, inactivity duration, and audio activity. These multimodal signals are fused to generate engagement trends across different time intervals of the session. This allows instructors to identify attention drops, interaction peaks, and session pacing issues.

Scenario 3: AI-Driven Teaching Suggestions
Based on detected engagement patterns and classroom dynamics, the agent provides AI-driven, context-aware teaching suggestions. These insights assist instructors in adjusting pacing, introducing interactive activities, or addressing classroom management challenges. The system does not enforce decisions but acts as an assistive decision-support tool.
